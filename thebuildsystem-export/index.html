The method

Three systems. Run together.

Most programs hand you a PDF and disappear. thebuildsystem runs training, nutrition and mindset as one loop — because a routine that ignores your head and your plate doesn't hold past week three.

01

Training

Progressive strength and conditioning blocks, built around your equipment and schedule — not a generic 12-week template.

02

Nutrition

Meal-by-meal calorie targets across three intensity levels, sport-nutrition style — with one guilt-free meal built in every weekend.

03

Mind

The part most coaches skip. Daily structure, journaling and check-ins for the fear-of-failure and no-motivation days — the ones that actually decide the outcome.
